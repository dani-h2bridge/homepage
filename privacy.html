Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 30, 2026
Last updated: March 30, 2026

1. About This App

Idea Vault is a link-saving service that helps you save, summarize, and organize content from across the web. It is operated by h2bridge, a small technology company focused on building useful products.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@h2bridge.xyz.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following information from your Google account:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account display name

We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or any other Google service data beyond the basic profile information listed above.

We do not collect payment card numbers directly. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which operates under its own security and privacy standards.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your email address and display name are used solely to create and identify your Idea Vault account. Specifically:

  • Your email address identifies your account and allows us to send transactional messages (e.g., password reset, billing).
  • Your display name is shown within the app interface.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

To request account deletion, email us at contact@h2bridge.xyz with the subject line "Delete my account". We will process the deletion within 30 days of receiving your request.

5. Cookies and Storage

We use browser local storage and session storage to maintain your authentication state between sessions. This is necessary for you to remain signed in across page loads.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

6. Third-Party Services

Idea Vault uses the following third-party services, each of which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — Database storage and authentication infrastructure
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Google OAuth — Sign-in via Google account

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services for more information about how they handle your data.
